Pricing FAQ

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The free tier requires no credit card and has no time limit.

What happens when I exceed my plan limits?

We'll notify you before you hit limits. Your workloads won't be stopped — you'll be prompted to upgrade.

Can I scale to zero?

Yes. On Free and Pro plans, idle deployments scale to zero instances and you pay nothing for compute during idle periods.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No contracts, no minimums. Cancel anytime. Enterprise plans have custom terms.
